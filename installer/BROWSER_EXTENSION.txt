TurboDM BROWSER EXTENSION — SETUP
=================================

The extension sends downloads started in the browser to TurboDM, catches videos on pages
(the "⬇ Download this video" button) and adds "Download with TurboDM" to the right-click menu.

Chrome / Edge / Brave / Opera:
  1. Type in the address bar:
       Chrome : chrome://extensions
       Edge   : edge://extensions
       Brave  : brave://extensions
  2. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right or bottom left corner).
  3. Click "Load unpacked".
  4. Select this folder:
       C:\Program Files\TurboDM\browser_extension
     (if you installed TurboDM in another folder, the browser_extension folder there)
  5. Pin TurboDM from the puzzle icon in the toolbar.

Notes:
  * While TurboDM is closed, downloads continue normally in the browser.
  * Click the extension icon to turn download capturing and the video grabber on or off.
  * When you uninstall TurboDM, also remove the extension with "Remove" on the browser's extensions page.
